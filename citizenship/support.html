怎么恢复购买？

以前买断过，换了手机或重新安装后：打开「我的 > 设置」，点「恢复购买」。要用当初购买时的同一个 Apple ID 登录 App Store。开通了家庭共享，家庭成员也可以这样恢复。

每天能免费练多少？

每天可以免费练一套题。还想再练，可以自己选择看一段广告，多换一套。一次买断后不限套数，也没有广告。买断不是订阅，不会每月扣钱。

怎么换界面语言、朗读语言、简体繁体？

在「我的 > 设置」里：界面语言可以选简体中文、繁體中文或 English，题目和答案的中文跟着界面用简体或繁体；界面选 English 时，还可以另外选中文用简体还是繁体。朗读语言可以选英文、普通话或粤语。

怎么换回标准模式或长辈模式？

在「我的 > 设置」里随时切换。长辈模式字更大、按钮更大。

我该练 2025 版还是 2008 版？

考哪一版，看你递交 N-400 的日期，以美国移民局（USCIS）的公告为准。应用里两版都有。

我那里的参议员、众议员是谁？

这几道题的答案每个地方不一样。在应用里填上你的邮编，就会显示你那里的答案。名单会自动更新；邮编只存在你的手机上。

没网能用吗？

题目和朗读都装在应用里，没网也能练。看广告和恢复购买需要联网。

How do I restore my purchase?

If you bought the app before and have a new phone or reinstalled it, open Me > Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Family Sharing is on, so family members can restore it the same way.

How much can I practice for free?

One free practice set every day. For more, you can choose to watch an ad for an extra set. The one-time purchase unlocks unlimited sets with no ads. It is not a subscription, so there are no monthly charges.

How do I change the app language, read-aloud language or Chinese script?

In Me > Settings: the app language can be Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or English, and Chinese in questions and answers follows that choice. With the app in English, you can separately choose Simplified or Traditional for the Chinese. The read-aloud language can be English, Mandarin or Cantonese.

How do I switch between Standard and Senior mode?

Switch any time in Me > Settings. Senior mode has larger text and bigger buttons.

Should I study the 2025 test or the 2008 test?

It depends on the date you filed Form N-400; follow the USCIS announcement. Both tests are in the app.

Who are my senators and representative?

These answers depend on where you live. Enter your ZIP code in the app to see your answers. The list updates automatically, and your ZIP code stays on your phone.

Does it work offline?

Questions and audio are built into the app, so you can practice without internet. Watching an ad and restoring a purchase need a connection.
