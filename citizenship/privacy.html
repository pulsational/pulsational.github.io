美国入籍助手 隐私政策

生效日期

2026 年 11 月 12 日

我们是谁

「美国入籍助手」（英文名 Citizenship Helper: US Test）是一款帮助准备美国入籍公民考试和面试的 iPhone 应用，由个人开发者 Fangyi Wang 开发。下文的「我们」指开发者。

一句话说明

不用注册账号。我们自己没有服务器，不收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话。

你的学习记录和设置只存在你自己的手机上。

应用里有两个第三方服务会收集一部分数据：Google AdMob（广告）和 RevenueCat（处理一次买断）。下面逐项说明。

只存在你手机上的数据

设置（界面语言、朗读语言、简繁、长辈模式等）、练习记录、错题、你选定要背的答案、每天的免费次数、是否已经买断。

你填写的邮编和州：只用来在手机上找出你那里的参议员、众议员、州长，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

这些数据不会上传给我们。删除应用，这些数据就会一起从手机上删除。

Google AdMob（广告）

免费版里，只有你自己点了「看一段广告」，才会播放一段激励广告来换一套题。应用里没有横幅广告，也没有插屏广告。买断以后不再显示任何广告。

播放广告时，Google AdMob 可能收集：设备标识符（你允许追踪时，包括苹果的广告标识符 IDFA）；根据 IP 地址推断的大致位置；与广告的互动（例如看了哪些广告、是否点击）；诊断数据（崩溃记录、性能数据）。这些数据用于投放和衡量广告、分析和防止作弊。

第一次播放广告之前，在法律要求的地区会先请你选择是否同意个性化广告（Google 的同意窗口），然后苹果会问你是否「允许追踪」。如果你不同意或不允许追踪，只会显示非个性化广告。你随时可以在 iPhone 的「设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 跟踪」里更改。

Google 如何使用这些数据：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

RevenueCat（处理买断）

一次买断由苹果收款，我们和 RevenueCat 都拿不到你的银行卡或付款信息。

RevenueCat 帮我们确认你是否已经买断、处理「恢复购买」。它使用一个随机生成的匿名 ID，不和你的姓名、邮箱关联；记录的是购买信息（买了什么、什么时候、苹果的交易凭证），用于让买断生效和做统计，不用于追踪你。

RevenueCat 的隐私政策：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

其他联网

应用会从 GitHub Pages（pulsational.github.io）下载公开的官员名单和照片清单，只是下载文件，不会发送你的邮编或其他个人信息。和所有网站一样，托管方可能记录访问的 IP 地址。

你点开参议院、众议院、州长协会、移民局等网站的链接时，会在浏览器里打开外部网站，适用这些网站自己的隐私政策。

儿童

本应用面向准备入籍考试的成年人，不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

你的选择

不想看广告：不点「看一段广告」即可，或者一次买断。

不想被追踪：在苹果的追踪窗口里选「要求 App 不跟踪」，或者在 iPhone 设置里关掉。

删除数据：删除应用即可删除手机上的全部数据。我们没有保存你的数据，所以没有需要我们替你删除的内容。Google 和 RevenueCat 处理的数据，请参照它们各自的隐私政策。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会在这个网页上公布新版本并更新生效日期。

Citizenship Helper Privacy Policy

Effective date

November 12, 2026

Who we are

Citizenship Helper: US Test (Chinese name 美国入籍助手) is an iPhone app for preparing for the U.S. citizenship civics test and interview. It is made by an individual developer, Fangyi Wang. "We" below means the developer.

In short

No account is needed. We have no servers of our own and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Your study records and settings stay on your own phone.

Two third-party services in the app collect some data: Google AdMob (ads) and RevenueCat (the one-time purchase). Details are below.

Data that stays on your phone

Settings (app language, read-aloud language, Simplified or Traditional Chinese, Senior mode and so on), practice history, missed questions, the answers you chose to learn, your daily free sets, and whether you have bought the app.

The ZIP code and state you enter are used only on your phone to find your senators, representative and governor. They are not sent to us or to any third party.

None of this is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this data from your phone.

Google AdMob (ads)

In the free version, a rewarded video ad plays only when you tap to watch one in exchange for an extra practice set. There are no banner ads and no interstitial ads. After the one-time purchase, no ads are shown at all.

When an ad plays, Google AdMob may collect: device identifiers (including Apple's advertising identifier, IDFA, if you allow tracking); approximate location inferred from your IP address; interactions with ads (for example which ads were shown and whether you tapped them); and diagnostics (crash logs and performance data). This is used to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud.

Before the first ad, where the law requires it you will be asked whether you consent to personalized ads (Google's consent form), and then Apple will ask whether to allow tracking. If you do not consent or do not allow tracking, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

RevenueCat (the one-time purchase)

Payment for the one-time purchase is handled by Apple. Neither we nor RevenueCat receive your card or payment details.

RevenueCat lets the app check whether you have bought it and handles Restore Purchases. It uses a randomly generated anonymous ID that is not linked to your name or email. It records purchase information (what was bought, when, and Apple's transaction receipt) to make the purchase work and for statistics. It is not used to track you.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Other network use

The app downloads a public list of elected officials and a photo list from GitHub Pages (pulsational.github.io). This only downloads files; your ZIP code and other personal information are not sent. Like any website host, GitHub may log the IP addresses that visit.

Links to the Senate, the House, the National Governors Association, USCIS and similar sites open in your browser, and those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the citizenship test and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

To avoid ads, simply don't tap to watch one, or make the one-time purchase.

To avoid tracking, choose "Ask App Not to Track" when Apple asks, or turn it off in iPhone Settings.

To delete your data, delete the app; everything on your phone is removed. We do not keep your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. For data processed by Google and RevenueCat, see their privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
